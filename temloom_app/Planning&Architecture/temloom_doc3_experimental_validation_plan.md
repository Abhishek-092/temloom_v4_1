## 1. Dataset Generation, Independent Ground-Truth & Anti-Leakage Protocol (Sections 1 & 3.1)

### 1.1 Internal Dataset Generation & Ground-Truth Independence
1. **Self-Contained Air-Gapped Generation:** All telemetry (`Schemas 1–10`), fault-injection logs (`Schema 11`), ground-truth incident labels (`Schema 12`), and dual-collection RAG corpora (`runbooks` + operator-verified `incidents`) are generated and stored locally inside the TEMLOOM environment. Zero external cloud datasets or third-party APIs are required at training, calibration, or runtime.
2. **Independent Ground-Truth Construction (`Schemas 11 & 12`):**
   - Ground-truth labels (`sla_breach_occurred`, `sla_breach_onset_ms`, `recovery_utc_ms`) are computed out-of-band by the simulator/fault-injector harness (`fault_injector.py`) directly from the physical network state (e.g., 3 consecutive 15s steps where true link utilization $\ge 95.0\text{ Mbps}$, RTT $\ge 30.0\text{ ms}$, packet loss $\ge 1.0\%$, or queue drops $\ge 25.0\text{ pps}$; or 2 consecutive steps where [RFC 2439](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc2439/) flap penalty $\ge 2000$, OSPF SPF runs $\ge 3.5/\text{step}$, path asymmetry $\ge 0.45$, tunnel jitter $\ge 15.0\text{ ms}$, or IKEv2 DH retries $\ge 3.5$ with remaining SA life $\le 2.5\text{m}$).
   - **Never Derived From Predictions:** Model outputs (`Schema 13`) are never used to label ground truth.
   - **Strict Anti-Leakage Partitioning:** The runtime causal feature extractor (`extract_causal_features`) has zero access to `injected_fault_records` (`Schema 11`), `ground_truth_incident_labels` (`Schema 12`), or future time steps $t' > t$.
3. **Disjoint Seed, Temporal, Topology & Unseen-Fault Splits:**
   - **Training Split (`Seeds 101..105`):** 60 link runs + 30 routing runs + 30 tunnel runs used exclusively to fit supervised/unsupervised candidate models (`Histogram-GBDT`, `Random Forest`, `Recurrent Gated BiLSTM + Attention`, `Isolation Forest`).
   - **Validation / Calibration Split (`Seeds 201..205`):** 60 runs used to tune threshold hysteresis, initial conformal quantiles, and Dual-Engine Corroboration gates.
   - **Held-Out Standard Test Split (`Seeds 301..310`):** 120 link runs (`Scenarios A..L`), 60 routing runs (`Scenarios R1..R6`), and 60 tunnel runs (`Scenarios T1..T6`) — **240 held-out test runs**.
   - **Held-Out Unseen / Adversarial Split (`Seeds 401..410`):** 80 runs across **8 unseen adversarial fault families (`ADV-1` through `ADV-8`)** that never appear in the training or validation splits (including quadratic TCP-incast cascades `ADV-1`, sub-SLA step plateaus `ADV-2`, low-EF covariate shift `ADV-3`, permanent variance shifts `ADV-5`, falling-bandwidth latency blackholes `ADV-7`, and 4-minute periodic sawtooth waves `ADV-8`).
